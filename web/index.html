Analysis Overview

This customer segmentation application uses K-means behavioral clustering to identify distinct purchasing patterns within your customer base. By standardizing age, income, visit frequency, and average basket size, customers are segmented into three clearly defined groups. This empirical segmentation allows small businesses to craft tailored retention strategies, optimize promotional investments, and improve customer lifetime value.
